Admin User Manual

This guide explains how to run the Wavemakers wall-map experience from the admin dashboard. No prior knowledge of the project is required.

1. What this system is

Wavemakers is a WebAR experience for techNL. Visitors point a phone at a physical wall world map. Water ripples reveal pins. Each pin opens an impact story card.

There is also a browser simulator so you can try the same experience without a camera.

Page Who uses it Purpose
/ Public Landing: choose live map or simulator
/ar Public Live AR with phone camera and physical map
/ar-preview Public / admins Same UX without camera or wall map
/admin Admins only Cards, map calibration, ripples, story review
/share-story.html Public Submit an impact story for approval

2. How to sign in

  1. Open /admin in your browser.
  2. Enter the admin password.
  3. Select Sign in.

After a successful sign-in, the password is kept for the current browser session. It clears when you choose Sign Out or when the browser session ends.

To sign out: open the header menu (three lines on smaller screens) and choose Sign Out.

3. Admin overview

After login you see a header with three main panels. Opening one switches the workspace.

Panel Use it for
Edit Cards Create, edit, place, activate, or delete impact cards
Calibrate Map Align cities on the artistic map, and set the ripples origin
Review Submissions Approve or reject stories from the public form

The header menu also includes:

  • Share Story Form: opens the public submission page
  • Open AR Viewer: opens the camera-free preview
  • User Manual: this page
  • Sign Out

The Review Submissions button shows a badge with the number of pending stories.

4. First-time setup

Do this once when the wall map image is new or changed. Good calibration makes geocoded card pins land in the right places.

4.1 Calibration points

  1. Open Calibrate Map.
  2. Stay on the Calibration points tab.
  3. Type a city as City, Country (for example St. John's, Canada).
  4. Select Add point. A teal pin appears on the map.
  5. Drag that pin to the true spot on the artistic map.
  6. Repeat for at least two well-separated cities. Three or more improves accuracy.
  7. Select Save.

The status line shows the projection mode in use (for example linear, affine, or TPS). More well-placed points allow a stronger fit.

Tip: With fewer than two saved points, address geocoding still works but placement on the map is only approximate.

4.2 Ripples anchor

Ripples are the water effect that reveals pins in AR. You set where that effect starts on the map.

  1. Still in Calibrate Map, open the Ripples anchor tab.
  2. Choose Loop or Fade to edit that variant’s origin.
  3. Use the Active in AR viewer radios to pick which variant the public AR experience uses.
  4. Drag on the ripples preview to move the origin.
  5. Select Save.

5. Edit Cards

Open Edit Cards. You get a cropped wall map on one side and a cards list or edit form on the other.

5.1 Map controls

  • Scroll wheel: zoom in or out (about 1× to 6×).
  • Drag empty map: pan when zoomed in.
  • Drag selected pin: only while that card’s edit form is open.
  • Desktop hover: hover a pin or list item to preview the card on the map.
  • Phone / narrow screen: tap a pin to open that card in the sidebar.

5.2 Create a card

  1. Select New card.
  2. On the Details tab, fill in:
    • Title (required)
    • Company (optional)
    • Impact story (required)
    • Address (city / country text)
    • Active: leave checked if the card should appear in public AR
  3. Optional: open the Media tab and add an image URL and/or link URL.
  4. Place the pin:
    • Enter an address and select Geocode address, or
    • Drag the draft pin on the map.
  5. Select Save.

Geocode may snap to an existing nearby pin (same place name, or within about 500 m). A toast message confirms when that happens.

5.3 Edit or move a card

  1. Select a card from the list, or tap its pin on a small screen.
  2. Change fields in the form, and/or drag the selected pin.
  3. Select Save.

5.4 Active vs inactive

Uncheck Active to hide a card from the public AR experience. Inactive cards remain in the admin list so you can finish placement or hold a draft.

5.5 Several stories at one place

  1. Open an existing card.
  2. Select Add entry to this pin.
  3. Fill in the new title and story, then Save.

The pin shows that multiple entries exist. In the AR preview, visitors pick which entry to read. Dragging and saving moves all sibling cards together.

5.6 Delete a card

  1. Open the card.
  2. Select Delete and confirm.

6. Review Submissions

Public visitors send stories through the share form. Those land here as pending items. The header badge shows how many are waiting.

  1. Open Review Submissions.
  2. Select a pending item to read title, company, address, email, story, and links.
  3. Choose:
    • Approve: creates an inactive impact card, removes the submission, switches you to Edit Cards, and tries to geocode the address.
    • Reject: permanently deletes the submission after confirm.

Important: Approved cards start inactive and may sit near the map center until geocode or manual placement finishes. Open the card, fix the pin, check Active, then Save before it appears in AR.

7. Public story form

Share /share-story.html with people who should submit impact stories. You can also open it from the admin menu as Share Story Form.

Submissions do not appear in AR until an admin approves them and activates the resulting card (see section 6).

8. Verify in AR

  1. From the admin menu, open Open AR Viewer (or go to /ar-preview).
  2. Confirm ripples, pin positions, and card content look right.
  3. For the live wall: open /ar on a phone over HTTPS, point at the physical map, and aim the crosshair at pins.

Only Active cards appear for visitors. If a card is missing in AR, check that checkbox first.

9. Quick reference

Key ideas

  • Active: card is visible in public AR. Unchecked means admin-only draft.
  • Calibration: teaches the app how lat/lng maps onto your artistic wall image. Needed for accurate geocode placement.
  • Pins: positions on the map image. Saving a card stores that placement.
  • Ripples anchor: where the reveal effect starts; separate from card pins.

Common mistakes

  • Edited a pin or field but forgot Save.
  • Approved a submission and left the new card inactive.
  • Geocoded with fewer than two calibration points, so placement looks wrong.
  • Moved a calibration or ripples pin but did not Save that panel.
  • Expected a draft card to show in /ar while Active was off.

Suggested daily workflow

  1. Review Submissions: approve or reject.
  2. Edit Cards: place, activate, and polish new stories.
  3. Open AR Viewer: spot-check before visitors see the wall.
