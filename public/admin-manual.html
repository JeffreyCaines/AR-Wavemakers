1. What this system is

Wavemakers is techNL’s map of people and organizations in the NL tech community. Visitors share a story. After you approve it, a pin appears on the map.

On site, a phone camera lines up with the wall world map and ripples reveal pins. At home, visitors place a 3D map in the room. On a computer they get a 3D map in the browser.

2. How to sign in

/admin and /legacy-ar-preview require an admin email and password.

  1. Open /admin in your browser.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Select Sign in.

If this is your first sign-in with a temporary password, the page asks you to create a new password before you can use the dashboard. Failed sign-in stays on the same page and shows the error. Too many failed attempts are blocked for 15 minutes.

Your session stays for this browser tab. It clears when you select Sign Out, or when the session ends.

To change your password later, or to sign out, open the Account panel (called Accounts if you are the project admin) and use Change password or Sign Out.

Passwords must be 12 to 128 characters and include a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter, a digit, and a special character. They cannot match the account email.

3. Admin overview

After login, the header has panels. Opening one switches the workspace. /admin shows the 3D map from above.

The menu button at the top right has two links:

  • Open AR Viewer: opens /ar in a new tab. On a computer that is the 3D map. On a phone that is Select a Mode.
  • User Manual: this page

Review Submissions shows a badge with the number of pending stories.

4. Accounts

There are two admin roles. The project admin can create, delete, and reset wavemaker accounts. Wavemaker admins can use every other admin tool.

4.1 Your own account

Every admin has this panel. Wavemaker admins see it as Account Settings. It shows your email and role, plus two buttons:

  • Change password: enter your current password, then the new one twice.
  • Sign Out: ends the session and returns to the sign-in page.

4.2 Managing wavemaker admins

Only the project admin sees the account list and the management buttons.

  1. Open Accounts.
  2. Select Add new admin.
  3. Enter the new admin email and a temporary password that meets the password rules.
  4. Select Create account.

Give that email and temporary password to the new admin. When they sign in, they must create their own password before they can use the dashboard. Until they do, the list shows Temporary password next to their name.

To recover a forgotten wavemaker password, use Reset password and set a new temporary password. Delete removes an account and its access immediately.

New accounts are always wavemaker admins. You cannot delete the project admin account or reset its password from here. The project admin changes their own password with Change password.

5. First-time setup

Do this once when the map is new or the projection looks wrong. Good calibration puts geocoded pins in the right places. Use a wide desktop window so Calibrate Map is visible.

5.1 Calibration points

  1. Open Calibrate Map.
  2. Stay on the Calibration points tab.
  3. Type a city as City, Country (for example St. John's, Canada).
  4. Select Add point. A calibration pin appears on the map.
  5. Drag that pin to the true spot on the map. You can also nudge with arrow keys or WASD. Hold Shift for finer steps.
  6. Repeat for at least two well-separated cities. Three or more is better.
  7. Select Save.

The info button next to Map calibration shows the fit in use. One point shifts the default projection onto that spot. Two points give a linear fit. Three give an affine fit. Four or more use a thin-plate spline, which is the most accurate.

Select a point in the list, then Delete, to remove it. Remember to Save after you delete.

Tip: With fewer than two saved points, address lookup still works but placement on the map is only approximate.

5.2 Ripples config

Ripples are the water effect that reveals pins. You set where that effect starts on the map. On /admin the preview is the 3D map from above.

  1. Still in Calibrate Map, open the Ripples config tab.
  2. Select 3D model to watch the map’s built-in pulse, or Ripples shader for the custom overlay.
  3. For the custom overlay, select a Layer 7 cutoff (outer or inner edge) and drag on the map to move the origin.
  4. Rings, line width, max radius, timing, speed, softness, opacity, and color are a live preview only.
  5. Select Save to store the origin.

Only the origin is saved. The sliders help you judge the effect here, but the public viewers use their own fixed ripple settings. Changing a slider will not change what visitors see.

6. Edit Cards

Open Edit Cards. You get the 3D map from above on one side and a cards list or edit form on the other.

Toggle Individuals or Organizations at the top of the list. The map only shows pins for the selected type. A new card uses whichever tab is open.

6.1 Map controls

  • Scroll wheel: zoom in or out (about 1x to 6x).
  • Drag empty map: pan when zoomed in.
  • Drag selected pin: only while that card’s edit form is open.
  • Arrow keys / WASD: nudge the selected pin. Hold Shift for finer steps.
  • Desktop hover: hover a pin or list item to preview the card on the map.
  • Phone / narrow screen: tap a pin to open that card in the sidebar.

6.2 Create a card

  1. Pick Individuals or Organizations.
  2. Select New card.
  3. On the Details tab, fill in the same fields visitors use on the public form. For a person that is name, profession, current location, hometown, and the optional story questions. For an organization that is org name, industry, head office, and the optional story questions.
  4. Under Map placement, keep Active checked if the card should appear for visitors.
  5. Place the pin:
    • Enter an address and select Geocode address, or
    • Drag the draft pin on the map.
  6. Optional: open the Media tab. People get a photo URL. Organizations get logo, extra media, and YouTube.
  7. Select Save.

Media fields are links, not file pickers. Paste a public https link, or reuse the /api/uploads/… link from the visitor’s submission.

Geocode may snap to an existing nearby pin (same place name, or within about 500 m). A toast confirms when that happens. Saving then adds another entry at that pin.

After you change calibration, Recalculate pin from calibration moves the pin from the stored lat/lng. Save to keep it.

Consent and mailing-list checkboxes are read-only. They come from the original submission.

6.3 Edit or move a card

  1. Select a card from the list, or tap its pin on a small screen.
  2. Change fields in the form, and/or drag the selected pin.
  3. Select Save.

Inactive cards show as Inactive in the list.

6.4 Active vs inactive

Uncheck Active to hide a card from visitors. Inactive cards stay in the admin list so you can finish placement or hold a draft.

6.5 Several stories at one place

  1. Open an existing card.
  2. Select Add entry to this pin.
  3. Fill in the new person or organization, then Save.

The pin shows that several entries exist. Visitors pick which one to read. Dragging and saving moves every card at that pin together.

6.6 Delete a card

  1. Open the card.
  2. Select Delete and confirm.

7. Review Submissions

Public visitors send stories from the landing page. Those land here as pending items. The header badge shows how many are waiting.

  1. Open Review Submissions.
  2. Toggle Individuals or Organizations to match the form they used.
  3. Select a pending item. The detail view shows the submitted fields, photos, and links.
  4. On a phone, tap an item to open the detail overlay. Use the back button to return to the list.
  5. Select:
    • Approve: creates an inactive card, removes the submission, switches you to Edit Cards, and tries to geocode the address (current location for people, head office for organizations).
    • Reject: permanently deletes the submission after you confirm.

A Homograph detected label means a lookalike character was found in an email or URL and replaced with Latin text. Check those fields before you approve. The detail view shows what was swapped.

Important: Approved cards start inactive. They may sit near the map center until geocode or manual placement finishes. Open the card, fix the pin, check Active, then Save before it appears for visitors.

8. Public story form

Visitors submit from the landing page. On a computer they select Individual or Organization. On a phone they tap Share Your Story, then select Individual or Organization. A first-visit disclaimer notes this is not a production project.

Submissions do not appear on the map until you approve them and turn the card Active (see section 7).

9. Check the public experience

Only Active cards appear for visitors. Check that box first if a pin is missing.

Every public viewer has a header toggle for Organization or People, plus search and filters (profession for people, industry for organizations).

  1. From the admin menu, open Open AR Viewer. On a computer that is the 3D map. On a phone that is Select a Mode.
  2. On the 3D map (/ar on a computer, or Launch Map from /): left drag pans (the view stays on the map), right drag pivots, scroll zooms. Hover a pin for a popup. Click to open the details shelf. Use Replay reveal to watch the pulse again.
  3. For the live wall: open /ar on a phone over HTTPS, select On Site, and align the camera with the map. Aim the crosshair at a pin and hold about 2 seconds.
  4. For at-home AR: open /ar, select At Home. Tap a surface to place the map. Use the place button to remove it and place again. Two-finger tap recenters.
  5. For a camera-free check of the wall-map AR, open /legacy-ar-preview. It is password-gated.
